Baby AI Assistant — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 13, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

The short version

Baby AI Assistant keeps your family's baby-care log on your device by default. There are no email or password accounts — you never sign up or sign in. We show no advertising, do no cross-app tracking, and never sell data. The only times anything leaves your phone are listed below: if you create or join a family with an invite code, the family's shared log syncs to our server so the people you invite can see and add to it; purchases are handled by your app store and RevenueCat; and if you use an AI feature — voice logging, a summary, or a chat message — the audio or a small structured extract of your log is sent to create the result.

What the app stores on your device

  • Child profiles you create — a name you choose, a birth date, and whether the child is a boy or a girl. Use a nickname if you prefer; the app works the same.
  • Caregiver profiles — a name and a role (for example Mom, Grandma, Dad), so each entry shows who logged it.
  • Your care log: feedings (nursing side and duration, bottle amounts), sleep, diaper changes, pumping, medicine, temperature, solid foods, and notes — each with its time and the caregiver who recorded it.
  • Growth measurements (weight, length/height, head circumference) and vaccination records you mark as given.
  • App settings, such as your units (metric or imperial).
  • A clearly labeled SAMPLE demo family, so you can see how the app works before entering anything real.

All of this stays in the app's storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it. The only exception is Family Sync, and only if you choose to use it:

Family Sync — optional, off until you join a family

If you use the app on your own, your log is not stored on our server at all. If you create a family, or join one with its invite code, the app syncs the family's shared log to our server so every member sees the same record:

  • What syncs: the shared family log — child profiles, care entries, growth measurements, and vaccination records — the same things listed above.
  • When: only after you create or join a family. Joining is always your choice, using the family's invite code.
  • Who can see it: only the family members who joined with that invite code — the people you invite. Members can read the shared log, and caregivers in the family can add to it.
  • Identity, still without accounts: there is still no email, username, or password. Your install is identified by a random member ID the app creates on your device, plus a member credential our server issues to that install; the server stores only a hash of the credential.

AI features — voice logging, summaries and chat

The app can log an entry from your voice, turn your own log into plain-language summaries (for example, how feeding and sleep looked over the last two weeks), and it includes an AI chat assistant you can ask questions about your log in plain language. These features use our server only when you choose to use them — recording a voice log, tapping to generate a summary, or sending a chat message. The processing runs on our backend with Google's Gemini AI:

  • Voice logging: when you record a spoken entry, the audio is sent over HTTPS to our backend, which passes it to Google Gemini to transcribe and structure. You get back a draft entry to review first — nothing is saved to your log until you confirm it.
  • A minimized, structured extract of the log is sent over HTTPS to our backend, which passes it to Google Gemini solely to generate the summary or chat answer. The extract contains entry types, times, amounts, recent entries, and your child's age in months — no names, and no photos (the app does not have photos at all). When you chat, your question is sent along with the extract, solely so the assistant can answer it.
  • Your voice audio, the extract and your question are used only to produce your draft, summary or answer. They are not sold, not used for advertising, and not retained beyond processing.
  • If you never use these features, nothing is sent.
  • Summaries and chat answers are educational information drawn from your own records — they are not medical advice, and the app does not diagnose anything. See the Terms of Use.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no cross-app tracking, and no data selling — ever.
  • No analytics SDKs. The only usage information we can see is the anonymous, aggregate statistics Apple provides to all developers (for example, total installs and crash counts); it is not linked to you or your child.
  • No diagnosis. Baby AI Assistant is an organizational and educational tool for caregivers; it is not a medical device, and we are not a healthcare provider. We do not claim HIPAA compliance; instead, the app keeps your family's records on your own device by default, and syncs them to our server only if you choose Family Sync.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed for us by RevenueCat. We receive subscription status (which plan, whether it is active) — never your card details. Purchases are linked to your store account (your Apple ID or Google account) by the store and RevenueCat, not to any account in this app, because the app has no accounts.

Children

Baby AI Assistant is made for adults — parents and other caregivers — who record information about the children in their care. It is not directed at children, and children do not create accounts or use the app themselves. Only record information about a child if you are that child's parent or guardian, or have the parent's or guardian's permission.

Your choices & contact

Your data is under your control. In the app's Settings, under Danger zone, "Delete my account & data" erases your family membership on our server (if you joined a family) and then everything on this device; "Delete all my data (device only)" erases just this device. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data. You can also request deletion by email — see our account & data deletion page for both paths. Questions or requests: info@annizon.com.
