Baby AI Assistant — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

The short version

Baby AI Assistant keeps your family's baby-care log on your device. There are no accounts — you never sign up or sign in — and in this version we do not operate a server copy of your log. We show no advertising, do no cross-app tracking, and never sell data. The only time anything leaves your phone is listed below: purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat, and if you tap to generate an AI summary, a small structured extract of your log is sent once to create that summary.

What the app stores on your device

  • Child profiles you create — a name you choose, a birth date, and whether the child is a boy or a girl. Use a nickname if you prefer; the app works the same.
  • Caregiver profiles — a name and a role (for example Mom, Grandma, Dad), so each entry shows who logged it.
  • Your care log: feedings (nursing side and duration, bottle amounts), sleep, diaper changes, pumping, medicine, temperature, solid foods, and notes — each with its time and the caregiver who recorded it.
  • Growth measurements (weight, length/height, head circumference) and vaccination records you mark as given.
  • App settings, such as your units (metric or imperial).
  • A clearly labeled SAMPLE demo family, so you can see how the app works before entering anything real.

All of this stays in the app's storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

AI summaries

The app can turn your own log into plain-language summaries (for example, how feeding and sleep looked over the last two weeks). This is the one feature that uses our server, and only when you tap to generate a summary:

  • A minimized, structured extract of the log is sent over HTTPS to our backend, which passes it to our AI provider solely to generate the summary. The extract contains entry types, times, amounts, and your child's age in months — no photos (the app does not collect photos of your child).
  • The extract is used only to produce your summary. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not retained beyond processing.
  • If you never tap to generate a summary, nothing is sent.
  • Summaries are educational information drawn from your own records — they are not medical advice, and the app does not diagnose anything. See the Terms of Use.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no cross-app tracking, and no data selling — ever.
  • No analytics SDKs. The only usage information we can see is the anonymous, aggregate statistics Apple provides to all developers (for example, total installs and crash counts); it is not linked to you or your child.
  • No diagnosis. Baby AI Assistant is an organizational and educational tool for caregivers; it is not a medical device, and we are not a healthcare provider. We do not claim HIPAA compliance; instead, the app is designed so that your family's records stay on your own device in the first place.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store and managed for us by RevenueCat. We receive subscription status (which plan, whether it is active) — never your card details. Purchases are linked to your Apple ID by Apple and RevenueCat, not to any account in this app, because the app has no accounts.

Children

Baby AI Assistant is made for adults — parents and other caregivers — who record information about the children in their care. It is not directed at children, and children do not create accounts or use the app themselves. Only record information about a child if you are that child's parent or guardian, or have the parent's or guardian's permission.

Your choices & contact

Your data lives on your device, under your control. You can erase everything at any time with "Delete all my data" in the app's Settings, or by deleting the app itself. Questions or requests: info@annizon.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
