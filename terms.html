What this app is — and is not

Baby AI Assistant helps parents and caregivers keep one shared log of a baby's or young child's day — feedings, sleep, diapers, pumping, medicine, temperature, growth, and vaccinations — and turns those records into plain-language summaries and a doctor report. It is an organizational and educational tool only. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, it does not replace your pediatrician, and nothing in the app — including AI summaries — is medical advice. Always discuss your child's health, feeding, medication, and vaccination questions with your own pediatrician. If you think your child may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

Reference information

  • Growth charts: the percentile bands in the app are simplified, WHO-based approximations for orientation only — not a medical assessment. Your pediatrician has the full charts and your child's complete picture.
  • Vaccine schedules: the US (CDC) and China (NIP) schedules shown in the app are reference data to help you keep track. Schedules change, and the right timing for your child can differ — confirm what is due with your pediatrician, and rely on your child's official vaccination record, not this app, as the document of record.
  • Doctor report: the report is generated from the entries you and your caregivers logged. Check it for accuracy before sharing it — it can only reflect what was recorded.
  • AI summaries: summaries are generated from your own log and can be wrong or incomplete. Treat them as a starting point for your own questions, never as an instruction about your child's care.
