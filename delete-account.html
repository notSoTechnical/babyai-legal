Delete Your Account & Data

Baby AI Assistant (iOS & Android: com.annizon.bestbabyai) · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

Baby AI Assistant has no accounts — there is no email, username or password to delete. What you can delete is your data: everything the app keeps on your device, and — if you use Family sharing — the membership our server holds for you. You can do it at any time, in either of two ways. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. This page explains both options and exactly what gets deleted.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Baby AI Assistant and go to Settings.
  2. Under Danger zone, tap Delete my account & data.
  3. Read the explanation, tap Delete, then confirm with Yes, delete everything.

Your data is deleted right away: the app first deletes your family membership from our server (if you are in a family), then wipes this device and resets the app to its first-run state.

Settings also has a separate "Delete all my data (device only)" option. As its name says, that one erases only this phone — if you are in a family, your membership and the family's shared log stay on our server. Use Delete my account & data above if you want the server membership removed too.

Option 2 — Delete by email

Email info@annizon.com with the subject “Delete my Baby AI Assistant data”. If you still have the app installed, Option 1 is the sure path — it proves the data is yours instantly, using the member token stored on your device. If you no longer have the app, include your family's invite code and your member display name as shown in the app, and we will delete that membership and its server data. The app keeps no email addresses on file, so there is no account email to write from — the invite code and display name are how we find your membership.

What gets deleted

  • On your device: all child and caregiver profiles, the full care log (feedings, sleep, diapers, pumping, medicine, temperatures, foods and notes), growth measurements, vaccination records, your settings, and your family credentials. The app resets to its first-run state.
  • On our server (only if you used Family sharing): your membership in the family — your seat, your display name, and your member credential.
  • If you were the family's last remaining member: the family itself and its entire shared log are deleted too.
  • If you never joined a family: nothing of yours is on our server at all — your data lives only on your device, and deletion is entirely on-device.

If other members remain in your family: the family's shared log is the baby's care record, co-held by the remaining members, so it stays with them — including entries you logged into it. Your own membership and display name are removed from the member list, and everything on your device is still deleted in full.

Subscriptions

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. To stop future charges, cancel the subscription in your store's subscription settings as well. Purchase records themselves are held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies — we receive subscription status only, never your card details.

What we may retain

We may retain limited records where we are required to do so by law (for example, transaction records needed for tax or legal obligations). Everything else listed above is permanently deleted. For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.
